# ESRS E1-6 §44(b) — Gross location-based Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions

- **standard**: ESRS (2023-12 (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2772))
- **datapoint**: `ESRS_E1-6_gross_scope_2_location`
- **kind**: quantitative
- **reporting period basis**: fiscal_year
- **unit**: tCO2e (published to 0 dp)
- **consolidation boundary**: operational_control

## What the clause requires

Indirect greenhouse gas emissions from purchased electricity, computed with the location-based method using the published grid average emission factor.

## Methodology as declared

GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance, location-based method. Metered electricity consumption multiplied by the published national grid emission factor for the reporting year.

## Evidence the undertaking must hold

Inherited from the components of this figure.

## Lawful omissions

None. This datapoint has no permitted omission.

Anything else that prevents disclosure is an internal failure, not an omission: the report does not issue, and the reason code says so on the face of the statement.
