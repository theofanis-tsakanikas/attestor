# Annex IV §8 — Serious incidents recorded under post-market monitoring

- **standard**: EU_AI_ACT (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Annex IV)
- **datapoint**: `AIACT_ANNEX-IV-8_serious_incidents`
- **kind**: quantitative
- **reporting period basis**: fiscal_year
- **unit**: count (published to 0 dp)

## What the clause requires

Number of serious incidents reported under Article 73 during the monitoring period, as recorded in the provider's incident log.

## Methodology as declared

Count of incidents classified as serious under Article 3(49), within the monitoring period. Incidents under investigation are included and flagged; excluding them until classification completes would let a serious incident go unreported for as long as the review takes.

## Evidence the undertaking must hold

Documents of class `incident_log`; at least 1, covering the reporting period.

## Lawful omissions

- `E_NOT_APPLICABLE` — The datapoint does not apply to the undertaking's activities. (ESRS 1 §34)

Anything else that prevents disclosure is an internal failure, not an omission: the report does not issue, and the reason code says so on the face of the statement.
