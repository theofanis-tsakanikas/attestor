# Annex IV §3, Article 14 — Human oversight measures

- **standard**: EU_AI_ACT (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Annex IV)
- **datapoint**: `AIACT_ANNEX-IV-3_human_oversight`
- **kind**: narrative
- **reporting period basis**: point_in_time

## What the clause requires

Description of the human oversight measures built into the system, including the technical measures that make interpretation of outputs possible and the conditions under which a natural person may disregard, override or reverse an output.

## Methodology as declared

Drafted from the documented oversight procedure. Where the procedure describes an ability to override, the narrative states who holds it and what is recorded — an oversight measure nobody can exercise is not an oversight measure.

## Evidence the undertaking must hold

Documents of class `oversight_procedure`; at least 1, not necessarily within the reporting period.

## Lawful omissions

None. This datapoint has no permitted omission.

Anything else that prevents disclosure is an internal failure, not an omission: the report does not issue, and the reason code says so on the face of the statement.
