# Article 9(5) — Residual risks judged acceptable and still open

- **standard**: EU_AI_ACT (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 9)
- **datapoint**: `AIACT_ART-9_open_residual_risks`
- **kind**: quantitative
- **reporting period basis**: point_in_time
- **unit**: count (published to 0 dp)

## What the clause requires

Number of identified risks for which mitigation is complete and a residual risk remains, judged acceptable and communicated to the deployer.

## Methodology as declared

Count of entries in the risk register whose mitigation status is complete and whose residual rating is above negligible. Risks still under mitigation are excluded and counted separately; conflating them would understate what a deployer is being asked to accept.

## Evidence the undertaking must hold

Documents of class `risk_register_extract`; at least 1, covering the reporting period.

## Lawful omissions

None. This datapoint has no permitted omission.

Anything else that prevents disclosure is an internal failure, not an omission: the report does not issue, and the reason code says so on the face of the statement.
