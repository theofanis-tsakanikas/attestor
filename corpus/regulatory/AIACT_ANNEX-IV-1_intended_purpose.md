# Annex IV §1(a) — Intended purpose of the AI system

- **standard**: EU_AI_ACT (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Annex IV)
- **datapoint**: `AIACT_ANNEX-IV-1_intended_purpose`
- **kind**: narrative
- **reporting period basis**: point_in_time

## What the clause requires

General description of the AI system: its intended purpose, the provider, the version, and how the system interacts with hardware or software it is not itself part of.

## Methodology as declared

Drafted from the provider's model card and system documentation. Intended purpose is a legal characterisation with consequences for classification, so the narrative states what the documentation says and identifies where it is silent.

## Evidence the undertaking must hold

Documents of class `model_card`, `system_documentation`; at least 1, not necessarily within the reporting period.

## Lawful omissions

None. This datapoint has no permitted omission.

Anything else that prevents disclosure is an internal failure, not an omission: the report does not issue, and the reason code says so on the face of the statement.
