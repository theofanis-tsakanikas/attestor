# ESRS E1-6 §53 — Greenhouse gas intensity per net revenue

- **standard**: ESRS (2023-12 (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2772))
- **datapoint**: `ESRS_E1-6_ghg_intensity`
- **kind**: quantitative
- **reporting period basis**: fiscal_year
- **unit**: tCO2e/MEUR (published to 1 dp)
- **consolidation boundary**: value_chain

## What the clause requires

Total gross greenhouse gas emissions divided by net revenue, expressed in tonnes of CO2 equivalent per million euro.

## Methodology as declared

Total gross emissions (Scope 1 + location-based Scope 2 + Scope 3) over net revenue as reported in the financial statements. Both operands are disclosed in their own right, so the ratio is recomputable by a reader from the face of the statement.

## Evidence the undertaking must hold

Inherited from the components of this figure.

## Lawful omissions

- `E_PHASE_IN` — The standard permits phase-in of this datapoint for this undertaking. (ESRS 1 Appendix C (phase-in provisions))
- `E_NOT_MATERIAL` — The datapoint is not material for this undertaking. (ESRS 1 §29-33 (double materiality))

Anything else that prevents disclosure is an internal failure, not an omission: the report does not issue, and the reason code says so on the face of the statement.
