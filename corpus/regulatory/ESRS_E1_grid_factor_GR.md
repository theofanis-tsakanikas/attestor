# ESRS E1 AR 46 (disclosure of conversion factors) — Location-based grid emission factor, Greece

- **standard**: ESRS (2023-12 (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2772))
- **datapoint**: `ESRS_E1_grid_factor_GR`
- **kind**: quantitative
- **reporting period basis**: fiscal_year
- **unit**: tCO2e/MWh (published to 4 dp)

## What the clause requires

Residual-mix-free location-based emission factor for electricity consumed on the Greek interconnected grid, applied to location-based Scope 2 accounting for the reporting period.

## Methodology as declared

Location-based method per the GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance. The factor is the published national grid average for the reporting year; it is not interpolated and not forecast.

## Evidence the undertaking must hold

Documents of class `published_factor_source`; at least 1, covering the reporting period.

## Lawful omissions

None. This datapoint has no permitted omission.

Anything else that prevents disclosure is an internal failure, not an omission: the report does not issue, and the reason code says so on the face of the statement.
