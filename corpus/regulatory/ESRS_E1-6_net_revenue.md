# ESRS E1-6 §55 — Net revenue used to compute GHG intensity

- **standard**: ESRS (2023-12 (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2772))
- **datapoint**: `ESRS_E1-6_net_revenue`
- **kind**: quantitative
- **reporting period basis**: fiscal_year
- **unit**: MEUR (published to 2 dp)
- **consolidation boundary**: financial_control

## What the clause requires

Net revenue for the reporting period, as reported in the undertaking's financial statements, used as the denominator of the greenhouse gas intensity ratio.

## Methodology as declared

Net revenue as defined by the applicable financial reporting framework, taken from the audited financial statements for the same period without adjustment.

## Evidence the undertaking must hold

Documents of class `financial_statement`, `general_ledger_export`; at least 1, covering the reporting period.

## Lawful omissions

None. This datapoint has no permitted omission.

Anything else that prevents disclosure is an internal failure, not an omission: the report does not issue, and the reason code says so on the face of the statement.
