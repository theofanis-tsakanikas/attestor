# ESRS E1-6 §44(a) — Gross Scope 1 greenhouse gas emissions

- **standard**: ESRS (2023-12 (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2772))
- **datapoint**: `ESRS_E1-6_gross_scope_1`
- **kind**: quantitative
- **reporting period basis**: fiscal_year
- **unit**: tCO2e (published to 0 dp)
- **consolidation boundary**: operational_control

## What the clause requires

Direct greenhouse gas emissions from sources owned or controlled by the undertaking, in metric tonnes of CO2 equivalent, before any deduction for removals, carbon credits or contractual instruments.

## Methodology as declared

GHG Protocol Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard (revised edition), operational control approach. Global warming potentials from IPCC AR6 (100-year).

## Evidence the undertaking must hold

Documents of class `fleet_telematics_export`, `fuel_invoice`, `refrigerant_service_record`; at least 3, covering the reporting period.

## Lawful omissions

None. This datapoint has no permitted omission.

Anything else that prevents disclosure is an internal failure, not an omission: the report does not issue, and the reason code says so on the face of the statement.
