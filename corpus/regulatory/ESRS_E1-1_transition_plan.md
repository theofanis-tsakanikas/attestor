# ESRS E1-1 §14-16 — Transition plan for climate change mitigation

- **standard**: ESRS (2023-12 (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2772))
- **datapoint**: `ESRS_E1-1_transition_plan`
- **kind**: narrative
- **reporting period basis**: fiscal_year

## What the clause requires

Narrative description of the undertaking's transition plan: its decarbonisation levers, the alignment of its targets with limiting warming to 1.5 °C, and the extent to which the plan is embedded in the general strategy.

## Methodology as declared

Drafted from the undertaking's board-approved transition plan and the ESRS E1-1 application requirements. Every assertion must cite retrieved evidence; the draft is reviewed and approved by a named human before the report is issued.

## Evidence the undertaking must hold

Documents of class `board_approved_transition_plan`, `capex_plan`; at least 1, covering the reporting period.

## Lawful omissions

- `E_NOT_MATERIAL` — The datapoint is not material for this undertaking. (ESRS 1 §29-33 (double materiality))

Anything else that prevents disclosure is an internal failure, not an omission: the report does not issue, and the reason code says so on the face of the statement.
