# What this is not

Attestor demonstrates how CSRD/ESRS and EU AI Act Annex IV reporting can be made verifiable.
It is a working system and the claims in the README are asserted by the test suite on every
push. It is not a filing, and it is not advice.

- **The undertakings are invented.** Helios Logistics, Aegis Foods and Lumen Advisory do not
  exist. Any resemblance to a real company is accidental.
- **Every figure is synthetic.** The lake is generated by `pipelines/seed/generate.py` from
  recorded targets. No real emissions, revenue or evaluation data is present, and none has
  ever been.
- **Nothing here has been through an assurance provider.** The documents this system renders
  are shaped like a sustainability statement and a technical file. They have not been audited,
  and they must not be submitted to a regulator as though they had.
- **The reason codes are real, the disclosures are not.** `E_PARTIAL_BOUNDARY` cites ESRS
  E1-6 §44(c) because that is what the standard says. It does not follow that the figure
  beside it means anything outside this repository.

One exception, and it is the point of the project: tenant `lumen` documents **Attestor
itself**, and the robustness figures in its Annex IV — the injection block rate and the
false-positive rate — are measured by running this repository's own scanner over its own
labelled corpus. Those two numbers are real. `attestor evidence export --check` fails the
build when they stop matching what the harness produces.
