# Supplier attestation — Nordhaven Haulage AS

Reporting period 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026. Submitted 14 February 2027 in response to
the undertaking's value-chain data request under ESRS E1-6 §44(c), Category 4 (upstream
transportation and distribution).

## Attested activity

Nordhaven Haulage AS confirms it operated 412 subcontracted road movements on behalf of Helios
Logistics during the period, covering the Oslo–Gothenburg and Gothenburg–Hamburg corridors. Fuel
consumption for those movements is reported to the undertaking monthly through the procurement
ledger and is not restated here.

## Basis of preparation

Emissions were calculated on a fuel-based method using supplier-specific consumption records.
Where a movement was subcontracted onward, the attestation covers only the leg operated
directly.
