# Transition plan for climate change mitigation

Approved by the Board of Helios Logistics S.A. on 12 December 2025, minute 2025/11-04.

## Scope and governance

This plan covers the undertaking's own operations and the upstream and downstream transport
services it contracts. It is reviewed annually by the Board's Sustainability Committee, which
reports to the full Board twice a year. The Chief Operating Officer is accountable for
delivery; remuneration for the executive committee is linked to the Scope 1 and Scope 2
trajectory below from the 2027 financial year.

## Alignment with 1.5 °C

The undertaking's targets are set against a 1.5 °C pathway using the sectoral decarbonisation
approach for road freight. Absolute Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions are to fall by 42% against
the 2024 base year by 2030, and by 90% by 2040, with residual emissions neutralised only after
that reduction has been achieved. The base year has been recalculated once, following the 2025
acquisition of the Thessaloniki depot, and the recalculation is disclosed in the prior-period
note.

## Decarbonisation levers

Four levers carry the reduction, in order of contribution:

1. **Fleet replacement.** 340 diesel rigid vehicles are replaced with battery-electric
   equivalents between 2026 and 2030, beginning with the urban distribution fleet where duty
   cycles are shortest. This lever accounts for 61% of the planned Scope 1 reduction.
2. **Depot electrification.** Gas heating at seven depots is replaced with heat pumps by 2028.
3. **Renewable electricity.** A ten-year power purchase agreement signed in March 2026 covers
   an estimated 78% of forecast site consumption from 2027.
4. **Load factor and routing.** Operational measures raise average load factor from 71% to
   79%, avoiding an estimated 4,100 tonnes CO2e a year by 2030.

## Locked-in emissions and remaining exposure

The undertaking holds no coal, oil or gas assets. Locked-in emissions arise from the 180
vehicles whose leases run to 2032 and which are not replaceable within the plan period; these
are disclosed as a limitation rather than presented as abated.

## Embedding in the general strategy

The plan is embedded in the undertaking's financial planning: the capital expenditure required
is approved in the 2026–2030 capital plan (document CAPEX-HEL-2026), and no vehicle purchase
outside the plan may be approved without Board consent. The undertaking is not excluded from
the EU Paris-aligned Benchmarks.
