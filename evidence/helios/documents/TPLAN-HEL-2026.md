# Transition plan for climate change mitigation

Approved by the Board of Helios Logistics S.A. and recorded in the minutes of its December
meeting.

## Governance

The plan is reviewed annually by the Board's Sustainability Committee, which reports to the
full Board twice a year. The Chief Operating Officer is accountable for delivery. Executive
remuneration is linked to the emissions trajectory set out below from the following financial
year onward, so that responsibility for the plan sits with the people who direct operations
rather than with a reporting function.

## Alignment with limiting warming to one and a half degrees

Targets are set against a pathway consistent with limiting warming to one and a half degrees,
using the sectoral decarbonisation approach for road freight. The plan commits the undertaking
to an absolute reduction in direct and purchased-energy emissions against its stated base
year, and to a deeper reduction by the middle of the following decade. Residual emissions are
to be neutralised only after that reduction has been delivered, and neutralisation is not
counted towards the reduction itself.

The base year has been recalculated once, following the acquisition of a northern distribution
depot. The recalculation and its cause are disclosed in the prior-period note rather than
applied silently.

## Decarbonisation levers

Four levers carry the reduction.

**Fleet replacement.** Diesel rigid vehicles are replaced with battery-electric equivalents
over the plan period, beginning with the urban distribution fleet where duty cycles are
shortest and charging is least constrained. This is the largest single contributor.

**Depot electrification.** Gas heating at the undertaking's depots is replaced with heat
pumps, sequenced so that no site loses heating capacity during a winter period.

**Renewable electricity.** A long-term power purchase agreement signed during the reporting
period covers the majority of forecast site consumption from the following year.

**Load factor and routing.** Operational measures raise average load factor and reduce empty
running. This lever requires no capital and is the first to deliver.

## Locked-in emissions

The undertaking holds no coal, oil or gas assets. Locked-in emissions arise from vehicles
whose leases extend beyond the plan period and which cannot be replaced within it. These are
disclosed as a limitation on the plan rather than presented as abated.

## Embedding in the general strategy

The plan is embedded in financial planning. The capital expenditure it requires is approved
within the undertaking's capital plan, and no vehicle purchase falling outside the plan may be
approved without Board consent. The undertaking is not excluded from the EU Paris-aligned
Benchmarks.
