# Transition plan — annex on value chain dependency and assumptions

Approved by the Board with the transition plan and forming part of it.

## Where the plan depends on the value chain

A material part of the undertaking's reduction does not sit within its own operations. Three
dependencies are recorded here because a plan that presents them as internal is a plan that
overstates what the undertaking controls.

**Subcontracted haulage.** A substantial share of long-distance movements is performed by
contracted hauliers. The undertaking can specify vehicle standards at tender and has begun to
do so, but it cannot replace another operator's fleet. The reduction attributed to this
dependency is therefore contingent on the hauliers' own transition, and is disclosed as such.

**Grid decarbonisation.** The benefit of fleet electrification depends on the emissions
intensity of the electricity supplied. The power purchase agreement reduces but does not
remove this dependency, because charging at third-party locations remains on grid supply.

**Charging availability on trunk routes.** Public charging capacity on the undertaking's
principal corridors is outside its control. Where capacity does not arrive, vehicles remain
diesel for longer than the plan assumes.

## Assumptions the plan rests on

The plan assumes that vehicle availability follows manufacturer commitments already contracted,
that depot electrical capacity can be upgraded within the periods agreed with the network
operator, and that no acquisition materially changes the consolidation boundary. Each
assumption is reviewed annually by the Sustainability Committee, and a failed assumption is
reported to the Board as a revision to the plan rather than absorbed into the next year's
figures.

## Treatment of the residual

The undertaking does not intend to purchase offsets to close the gap between the plan and its
target. Where the plan does not reach the target, the shortfall is disclosed as a shortfall.
